Default reproduces the gallery bug: perspective camera + floor as receiver. The SVG intrinsic size matches the floor's polygon bbox (~17500 CSS-px square here); under perspective + steep rotX the layer projects to a huge tilted trapezoid that overruns the viewport's overflow:hidden ancestors. Switch to ortho or to the legacy ground-shadow path and the shadow stays put at every angle.
